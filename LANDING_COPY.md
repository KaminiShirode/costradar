## FAQ

**Q: Is it actually free?**
A: Yes. No credit card, no account. You see your full audit results before we ask for anything.

**Q: How accurate is the pricing data?**
A: We verify every price against official vendor pricing pages at the start of each week. Every number in the engine has a source URL. If a price changes between updates, we'd rather flag it than guess — see PRICING_DATA.md in our public repo.

**Q: Who is Credex?**
A: Credex sources discounted AI infrastructure credits from companies that overforecast their AI usage — Cursor, Claude, ChatGPT Enterprise and others. When your audit shows large savings, a Credex advisor may reach out with a concrete offer.

**Q: What happens to my data?**
A: Your tool list and spend figures are stored to generate your audit and shareable link. If you submit your email, we send you the report and — for high-savings cases — introduce you to Credex. We don't sell data.

**Q: Can I share my audit with my CFO or co-founder?**
A: Yes — every audit gets a public URL. Personal info (email, company name) is stripped from the public version. Only tools and savings numbers are shown. Designed for exactly this sharing pattern.
