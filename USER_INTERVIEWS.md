# USER INTERVIEWS

*Three real conversations, 10–15 minutes each. Conducted during the week of May 6–12, 2025.*

*How to find people: DM founders on X, post in Indie Hackers Slack or r/startups asking for 10 minutes, message developers you know who manage team AI tools. The question to open with: "I'm building a free tool that audits AI tool spend for startup teams — would you give me 10 minutes to understand how you think about this cost?"*

*Note: these interviews must be real. Generic, contradiction-free answers are obvious. Every real conversation has at least one thing that surprises you or contradicts your assumptions — that's what makes them real.*
